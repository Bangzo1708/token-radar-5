How the value is worked out — read this before trusting the board

implied value = TVL × multiple × tier × growth × breadth × launch probability × 7%

  • Multiple — the protocol's category median mcap/TVL across the snapshot's own token-bearing protocols (128 comparables), winsorized at p5/p95. Categories with fewer than 5 comparables use the global median 0.214. Capped at 3.0. A median, not a mean: one rich token must not set the price.
  • Tier — (100M ÷ TVL)0.12, clamped 0.75–1.25. Diminishing returns on size: a giant TVL is often mercenary capital and earns a smaller multiple.
  • Growth — 1 + 0.5 × 7-day TVL change, clamped to −25%…+40%. A hot week lifts value by at most 20%; a cold week costs at most 12.5%.
  • Breadth — +8% per extra chain, up to +40%. Multi-chain reach is real, but the fifth chain adds as much as the second.
  • Launch probability — from how long the protocol has run with no token: a hump peaking at 540 days (~18 months), 25% at the edges, 75% at the peak. Too young and the team is still building; too old with no token and it likely never launches.
  • 7% — the airdrop share: the slice of a launch's value a farmer can plausibly claim. Change this one number and the whole board re-prices.

Every figure comes from the sealed snapshot (DefiLlama, 2026-11-14). No protocol name is hardcoded in the model. Protocols whose token already trades are comparables only — they price the board, they never sit on it. “Records no token” means the snapshot records none, not that none exists. Implied values are this entry's own claim, not a promise.
